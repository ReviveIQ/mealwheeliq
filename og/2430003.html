🍖 Herb-Crusted Lamb Chops with Roasted Carrots, Tomatoes & Fresh Basil

🍞 Shared by a Toaster Novice on MealWheelIQ

30 min · Medium · Mediterranean · 308 kcal per serving

Ingredients

  • 6 pieces lamb loin chops (about 4–5 oz each)
  • 4 medium carrots
  • 3 medium tomatoes
  • 1 piece jalapeño
  • 0.5 oz fresh basil
  • 0.25 oz fresh parsley
  • 4 stalks scallions (green tops only)
  • 3 cloves garlic cloves (for oil infusion only, removed before serving)
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp rosemary
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 0.25 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 piece lemon

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Peel carrots and cut on the bias into 1-inch pieces. Halve tomatoes and slice jalapeño into thin rounds, discarding seeds if you prefer less heat. Thinly slice green scallion tops. Set vegetables aside.
  2. Warm 3 tbsp olive oil with 3 smashed garlic cloves in a small saucepan over low heat for 2 minutes until fragrant, then remove and discard the garlic pieces — this carries the flavor without the FODMAP fructans. Pour 2 tbsp of the infused oil into a large oven-safe skillet and reserve the rest for the vegetables.
  3. Toss carrots with the remaining 2 tbsp infused oil, rosemary, thyme, red pepper flakes, and a pinch of black pepper on a sheet pan. Roast at 425°F until tender and caramelized at the edges, about 20 minutes, adding the halved tomatoes and jalapeño slices in the last 8 minutes.
  4. Pat lamb chops completely dry with paper towels and brush both sides with Dijon mustard, then press with black pepper. Heat the infused oil in the large skillet over high heat until shimmering. Sear lamb chops until a deep brown crust forms, about 3 minutes per side for medium-rare (internal temp 135°F). Transfer to a cutting board and rest 5 minutes.
  5. Deglaze the skillet with a squeeze of half the lemon, scraping up all browned bits over medium heat for 30 seconds to create a light pan jus.
  6. Arrange the roasted carrots and tomatoes on a serving platter, nestle the lamb chops on top, and drizzle with the pan jus. Scatter sliced scallion tops, torn fresh basil, and chopped parsley over everything. Finish with remaining lemon juice, taste, and season with a light pinch of black pepper — serve immediately while the lamb is hot.
