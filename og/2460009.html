🍗 Jamaican Jerk Whole Chicken with Roasted Bell Peppers & Cauliflower Rice

🍞 Shared by a Toaster Novice on MealWheelIQ

30 min · Medium · Jamaican · 529 kcal per serving

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs whole chicken, spatchcocked (backbone removed, flattened)
  • 2 piece scotch bonnet peppers (or habaneros)
  • 2 tsp ground allspice
  • 6 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 4 stalks green onion tops only (dark green parts), thinly sliced
  • 1 piece jalapeño, stemmed
  • 2 piece bell pepper, sliced into strips
  • 2 piece tomatoes, quartered
  • 1 head cauliflower head, grated or food-processed into rice
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic cloves, smashed (for oil infusion only — discard before eating)
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp thyme (dried)
  • 0.5 tsp cinnamon
  • 0.5 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 0.5 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 0.75 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp vinegar (white or apple cider)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F. Warm 2 tbsp olive oil with the 4 smashed garlic cloves in a small saucepan over low heat for 2 minutes until fragrant, then remove and discard all garlic pieces — this is your FODMAP-safe infused oil. Blend the scotch bonnet, jalapeño, green onion tops, fresh thyme leaves (stripped from 4 sprigs), allspice, brown sugar, paprika, cumin, cinnamon, red pepper flakes, black pepper, vinegar, and the garlic-infused oil together in a blender or using an immersion blender until a thick jerk paste forms, about 30 seconds.
  2. Pat the spatchcocked chicken completely dry with paper towels, then rub the jerk paste aggressively all over the skin and under the breast skin, pressing firmly to adhere. Season lightly with salt on the underside.
  3. Heat a large oven-safe skillet or cast-iron pan over high heat until smoking. Lay the chicken skin-side down and sear undisturbed until the skin is deeply golden and charred at the edges, about 4–5 minutes. Flip the chicken skin-side up, then scatter the sliced bell peppers and tomato quarters around it in the pan.
  4. Transfer the skillet to the 450°F oven and roast until an instant-read thermometer inserted into the thickest part of the thigh reads 165°F, about 22–25 minutes. The skin should be crackled and deeply caramelized. Let the chicken rest in the pan for 5 minutes.
  5. While the chicken rests, heat the remaining 2 tbsp olive oil in a separate non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the cauliflower rice and remaining 2 sprigs of fresh thyme leaves, toss to coat, and cook stirring occasionally until lightly golden and any moisture has evaporated, about 5–6 minutes. Season lightly with salt and black pepper, then discard the thyme sprigs.
  6. Carve the chicken into 3 portions (two breast halves and one leg-thigh piece for the largest portion, or divide evenly by weight). Taste the pan juices and spoon them over the chicken and roasted peppers. Serve each portion over a mound of cauliflower rice, garnished with the remaining sliced green onion tops and the roasted tomatoes alongside.
