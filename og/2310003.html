🍗 Seared Chicken with Sweet Potato & Green Bean Skillet

30 min · Easy · American · 541 kcal per serving

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs Chicken Breast
  • 1.25 lbs Sweet Potatoes
  • 10 oz Green Beans
  • 1 cup Cherry Tomatoes
  • 3 cups Spinach
  • 1.5 piece Avocado
  • 1 piece Lime
  • 4 stalks Green Onions (green tops only)
  • 0.25 cup Fresh Cilantro
  • 4 tbsp Garlic-infused olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1.5 tsp Paprika
  • 1 tsp Cumin
  • 0.5 tsp Chili powder
  • 0.75 tsp Black pepper
  • 0.5 tsp Salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Peel and cube sweet potatoes into ¾-inch pieces, trim green beans, and halve cherry tomatoes. Thinly slice green onion tops. Pat chicken breasts dry, then coat with 1½ tsp paprika, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp chili powder, and ½ tsp black pepper on both sides.
  2. Toss sweet potato cubes with 2 tbsp garlic-infused olive oil, a pinch of salt, and remaining black pepper on a large rimmed sheet pan. Roast at 425°F for 15 minutes until beginning to caramelize at the edges.
  3. Heat 2 tbsp garlic-infused olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Sear seasoned chicken breasts until golden brown, about 4 minutes per side, then transfer skillet to the oven and roast until an internal thermometer reads 165°F, about 10–12 minutes. Rest chicken on a cutting board for 5 minutes before slicing.
  4. Add sweet potatoes to the sheet pan alongside green beans after the initial 15-minute roast, toss with 1 tbsp olive oil, and return to oven for 10 minutes more until beans are blistered and potatoes are fork-tender with caramelized edges. Add cherry tomatoes in the final 3 minutes, just until they burst.
  5. Wilt spinach directly in the residual heat of the roasting pan by tossing it with the hot vegetables off the heat, stirring until just collapsed, about 1 minute.
  6. Slice rested chicken against the grain. Divide the sweet potato, green bean, tomato, and spinach mixture among three plates, top with sliced chicken, and finish each plate with sliced avocado, a squeeze of fresh lime juice, and a drizzle of remaining garlic-infused olive oil.
  7. Taste and season lightly with salt if needed, then scatter sliced green onion tops and fresh cilantro over each plate and serve immediately.
