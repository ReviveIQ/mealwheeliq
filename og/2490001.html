🌮 Mexican Chicken Bowl with Cauliflower Rice

🍞 Shared by a Toaster Novice on MealWheelIQ

30 min · Easy · Mexican · 235 kcal per serving

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs Chicken Breast
  • 1 head Cauliflower
  • 2 piece Bell Pepper
  • 1 piece Jalapeño
  • 2 piece Tomatoes
  • 4 stalks (green tops only) Green Onions
  • 1.5 oz Cheddar Cheese (aged, hard)
  • 3 tbsp Sour Cream
  • 3 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 2 piece Lime

Instructions

  1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil and 3 smashed garlic cloves in a large skillet over low heat for 90 seconds until fragrant, then remove and discard the garlic — this infuses the oil with flavor without the FODMAP fructans.
  2. Slice chicken breasts into 1-inch strips, then toss with 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp paprika, 1/2 tsp oregano, and a pinch of black pepper. Raise the heat to medium-high and sear chicken in the garlic-infused oil, turning once, until deeply golden and cooked through, about 5–6 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate and tent loosely with foil.
  3. Pulse cauliflower florets from the entire head in a food processor in two batches until they resemble coarse rice grains, about 8–10 pulses. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in the same skillet over medium-high heat, add cauliflower rice, and toss frequently until lightly toasted and just tender, about 5–6 minutes. Season with a pinch of cumin and black pepper, then transfer to a bowl.
  4. Heat remaining 1 tbsp olive oil in the skillet over medium-high heat. Add sliced bell peppers and diced jalapeño, tossing frequently until blistered and tender-crisp, about 4–5 minutes.
  5. Dice tomatoes and slice the green tops of the scallions. Add both to the skillet with the peppers, squeeze in the juice of 1 lime, and stir everything together over medium heat for 1–2 minutes until the tomatoes just begin to soften and the mixture is fragrant.
  6. Slice the rested chicken against the grain into strips. Divide cauliflower rice among three bowls and top with chicken strips, the pepper-tomato mixture, and a dollop of sour cream. Shred aged cheddar over each bowl.
  7. Taste each bowl, squeeze remaining lime over the top, and garnish with sliced green scallion tops. Serve immediately while the cauliflower rice is warm and the chicken is juicy.
