🍳 Skillet Chicken Breast with Roasted Potatoes, Spinach & Scallion-Herb Pan Sauce

🍞 Shared by a Toaster Novice on MealWheelIQ

30 min · Medium · American Comfort · 503 kcal per serving

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs chicken breast
  • 1 lbs Yukon Gold potatoes
  • 4 oz fresh spinach
  • 4 stalks green onions (green tops only, white bulbs discarded)
  • 0.25 cup fresh parsley
  • 0.33 cup Greek yogurt (lactose-free)
  • 3 cloves garlic cloves (for oil infusion only — removed before eating)
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 0.5 cup chicken broth (low-sodium)
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 0.5 tsp dried thyme
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper
  • 0.5 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Cut potatoes into 3/4-inch cubes, toss with 1 tbsp olive oil, paprika, thyme, salt, and pepper on a rimmed baking sheet, and roast until golden and crispy on the outside and tender inside, about 25 minutes, flipping once halfway through.
  2. Meanwhile, warm 3 smashed garlic cloves in 2 tbsp olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet over low heat for 2 minutes until fragrant — the oil will turn golden and aromatic. Remove and discard all garlic pieces, then raise heat to medium-high.
  3. Pat chicken breasts dry, season generously on both sides with salt, pepper, and a pinch of paprika. Sear in the garlic-infused oil until deep golden brown, about 4 minutes per side. Transfer skillet to the oven and roast alongside the potatoes until an instant-read thermometer reads 165°F, about 10 to 12 minutes. Rest chicken on a cutting board for 5 minutes.
  4. Return the skillet to medium heat over the stovetop. Pour in chicken broth, scraping up all browned bits from the bottom, and simmer until reduced by half, about 2 minutes. Whisk in Dijon mustard and remove from heat, then stir in Greek yogurt until smooth and creamy — do not boil after adding yogurt or it will break.
  5. Add the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil to a separate skillet over medium-high heat and wilt the spinach with sliced green scallion tops (green parts only), stirring frequently until just collapsed and bright green, about 2 minutes. Season lightly with black pepper.
  6. Slice chicken breast against the grain into thick pieces. Arrange over the roasted potatoes and wilted spinach, spoon the Dijon-yogurt pan sauce generously over the top, and garnish with chopped fresh parsley and remaining scallion greens. Taste, adjust seasoning, and serve immediately straight from the pan.
