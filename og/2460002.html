🫒 Greek Lemon-Herb Roasted Chicken with Potatoes & Tzatziki

🍞 Shared by a Toaster Novice on MealWheelIQ

30 min · Medium · Greek · 693 kcal per serving

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs Whole chicken, spatchcocked (backbone removed, flattened)
  • 10 oz Potatoes, cut into 1-inch wedges
  • 1.75 piece Tomatoes, halved
  • 0.84 piece Bell pepper, sliced into strips
  • 1.75 cups Spinach, fresh
  • 3.25 stalks Green onions, green tops only, thinly sliced
  • 1.25 oz Feta cheese, crumbled (use a hard, aged feta — low lactose)
  • 0.42 cups Greek yogurt, lactose-free plain
  • 0.42 piece Cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
  • 0.21 cups Fresh parsley, roughly chopped
  • 1.75 piece Lemon, juiced and zested
  • 1.75 oz Kalamata olives, pitted
  • 0.21 cups Fresh dill
  • 3.25 tbsp Olive oil
  • 3.25 cloves Garlic cloves, smashed (for infusing oil only — remove before eating)
  • 1.75 tsp Dried oregano
  • 0.84 tsp Paprika
  • 0.84 tsp Black pepper
  • 0.42 tsp Salt

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 450°F. Warm 3 tbsp olive oil with 4 smashed garlic cloves in a small saucepan over low heat for 2 minutes until fragrant, then remove and discard the garlic pieces — this infuses the oil with flavor without the FODMAP fructans.
  2. Toss potato wedges and bell pepper strips with 2 tbsp of the garlic-infused oil, 1 tsp oregano, paprika, and a pinch of black pepper on a large rimmed sheet pan, spreading in a single layer. Roast for 15 minutes until edges begin to turn golden.
  3. Meanwhile, pat the spatchcocked chicken dry, then rub all over with the remaining 1 tbsp garlic-infused oil, 1 tsp oregano, lemon zest of 1 lemon, half the lemon juice, 0.5 tsp salt, and black pepper — press the spices firmly into the skin.
  4. Push the par-roasted potatoes and peppers to the edges of the pan and nestle the chicken skin-side up in the center. Scatter halved tomatoes and kalamata olives around the chicken. Roast at 450°F for 22–25 minutes until the skin is deeply golden and a thermometer inserted in the thickest part of the thigh reads 165°F.
  5. While the chicken roasts, make tzatziki: stir together lactose-free Greek yogurt, grated squeezed cucumber, half the fresh dill, remaining lemon juice, and a pinch of black pepper in a small bowl until combined. Refrigerate until ready to serve.
  6. Remove the pan from the oven and scatter fresh spinach directly over the hot vegetables, tossing gently so it wilts in the residual heat, about 1 minute. Sprinkle crumbled feta, green onion tops, fresh parsley, and remaining dill over the entire pan.
  7. Carve the chicken and serve directly from the pan over the roasted vegetables and wilted spinach, with a generous dollop of tzatziki alongside. Taste and adjust with a final squeeze of lemon and crack of black pepper before serving.
