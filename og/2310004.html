🐟 Caribbean Jerk Salmon with Sweet Potato & Sautéed Greens

30 min · Easy · Caribbean · 505 kcal per serving

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs salmon fillets (skin-on)
  • 1.25 lbs sweet potatoes, peeled and cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 4 cups fresh spinach
  • 4 stalks green onions (green tops only, thinly sliced)
  • 2 piece lime
  • 1.5 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 0.33 cup fresh cilantro, roughly chopped
  • 1 piece avocado, sliced
  • 4 tbsp garlic-infused olive oil
  • 1.5 tsp allspice
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 0.25 tsp cinnamon
  • 0.5 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper
  • 0.5 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss sweet potato cubes with 1.5 tbsp garlic-infused olive oil, paprika, cinnamon, and a pinch of salt on a large sheet pan, spread in a single layer, and roast until golden and tender, about 22–25 minutes, flipping halfway through.
  2. Whisk together allspice, thyme, red pepper flakes, black pepper, grated ginger, the juice of 1 lime, and 1 tbsp garlic-infused olive oil in a small bowl to form the jerk paste. Pat salmon fillets dry, then rub the paste evenly over the flesh side of each fillet.
  3. Heat 1.5 tbsp garlic-infused olive oil in a large nonstick or cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Lay salmon skin-side up and sear, undisturbed, until a deep mahogany crust forms, about 4 minutes. Flip and cook skin-side down until the fish is just opaque through the thickest part, about 3–4 minutes more. Transfer to a plate and tent loosely with foil.
  4. Return the same skillet to medium heat and add the remaining 0.5 tbsp garlic-infused olive oil. Toss in spinach and the green tops of the scallions, stirring constantly until the spinach is wilted and bright green, about 2 minutes. Squeeze in the juice of half a lime and toss to coat.
  5. Taste everything and season lightly with salt and black pepper as needed. Divide the roasted sweet potatoes and wilted greens among three plates, lay a salmon fillet over each, and fan sliced avocado alongside. Finish with a squeeze of the remaining lime, scattered cilantro, and any remaining green onion tops. Serve immediately.
